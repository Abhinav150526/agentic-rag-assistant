RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

RAG has two main steps: ingestion and retrieval.

In ingestion, documents are loaded, split into chunks, converted into embeddings, and stored in a vector database.

In retrieval, the user question is converted into an embedding, compared with stored document embeddings, and the most relevant chunks are returned.

FAISS is a vector database used for similarity search.

Sentence Transformers can convert text into embeddings.
